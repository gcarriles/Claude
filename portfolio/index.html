Video breakdown

What happens in 30 seconds

Every second has a job. This is the structure I use for a testimonial-style ad, timed to the second.

this is the part most creators skip

  1. 0:00–0:02

    Hook

    A relatable line or visual that stops the scroll in the first two seconds.

  2. 0:02–0:08

    The problem

    The everyday frustration your customer already feels, shown in a real space.

  3. 0:08–0:22

    Product in use

    Close-up demo, texture shots and honest reactions. Your key benefits are said out loud and shown as on-screen text.

  4. 0:22–0:27

    Proof

    A before-and-after, a result or a "two weeks later" moment.

  5. 0:27–0:30

    Call to action

    A clear next step that matches your offer and landing page.
